# Credits: exercise demo clips

The demo clips in `videos/` are freely licensed works from Wikimedia Commons.
Each one was trimmed where noted, scaled to about 480 px wide, re-encoded as muted
H.264 MP4 (audio removed) and set to loop in the app. Those changes are the only
modifications. The app shows each clip's author and license under the video.

## Exercises without a clip

No freely licensed clip that clearly shows the right movement was found for these.
The app shows the exercise name and short form cues instead:

Dips, Lateral Raises, Hip Thrusts, Bulgarian Split Squats, Cable Kickbacks, Face Pulls,
Romanian Deadlift, Hip Abduction Machine, Straight-Arm Pulldown, Reverse (Rear Delt) Fly,
Seated Cable Row.

To add a clip for any exercise, open Routine, tap Edit, then Attach video. The clip is
stored only on your device, in IndexedDB.
